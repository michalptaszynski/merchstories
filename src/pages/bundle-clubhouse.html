A welcome that feels like membership
The Clubhouse bundles the pieces that make someone feel like they belong — a hoodie worth actually wearing, a tote for meetups, a mug for the ritual, a notebook for whatever the community is building together.
Built for a growing membership
Sized for a real member base: 40 of each item by default. Adjust any line with the Edit button before you order, as the community grows.
Consistent branding across every piece
Your logo is applied the same way across the hoodie, tote, mug and notebook, so the kit reads as one cohesive set instead of four unrelated giveaways. Our team confirms placement and sizing with a free digital mockup before production.
