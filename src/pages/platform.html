FAQ

Is the platform included with every order, or is it a separate product?

It’s included once you’re running merch through us regularly — there’s no separate software fee. Ask your account contact to get your team set up.

Can different offices or brands see only their own stock?

Yes. Each brand or market gets its own view of stock, budget, and orders, while admins can still see everything from one login.

How does design approval actually work?

Mockups are posted to a shared queue for your team to comment on and approve. Nothing goes to production until someone signs off in the dashboard.

What happens when stock runs low?

You set the threshold once per SKU. When stock crosses it, the dashboard flags it and you can trigger a reorder from the same screen.

Do you handle shipping to multiple countries?

Yes — we ship to 65 countries. Save an address book once and send a batch of orders out together instead of booking each one individually.

How do we get set up?

Tell us about your program and we’ll get back with free designs and a full quote within 24 hours, platform access included.
