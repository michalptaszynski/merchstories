A heavier, brushed-back fleece
The Custom Women Organic Cotton Hoodie is cut from a 320 GSM brushed-back organic cotton fleece — noticeably heavier than a standard tee, with a soft inner face that holds its shape wash after wash. The relaxed unisex fit, ribbed cuffs and hem keep it comfortable for long days at the office or layered up outdoors.
Built for your branding
Add your logo via embroidery, screen print, or DTG, whichever suits the design and budget best. Left chest is the classic placement, though front and back positions work too for bolder graphics. Our team will confirm placement and sizing with a free digital mockup before anything goes into production.
One hoodie, every team
A relaxed unisex cut means one size range covers the whole team, no separate men's/women's runs to manage. It reads as premium enough for client gifting and onboarding kits, while holding up to daily wear for warehouse or event staff.
Six colours to match your brand
Choose from Black, Anthracite, Burgundy, Eco Heather, Heritage Brown, or Honey Paper — a mix of true neutrals and warmer tones so your logo has the right backdrop whether you're going high-contrast or tonal.
Care that protects the print
Machine wash cold, inside out, with similar colours. Skip the bleach and tumble dry on low. Keep the iron off the print area entirely — heat direct on the design is the most common way a custom print gets damaged early.
