A uniform without the uniform feel
The Startup Uniform bundles the pieces a tech team already reaches for — a tumbler for the standup, a notebook for the sprint planning, a hoodie for the late pushes, and a powerbank for everything in between.
Built for a growing team
Sized for a full headcount: 40 of each item by default. Adjust any line with the Edit button before you order — useful as the team scales between funding rounds.
