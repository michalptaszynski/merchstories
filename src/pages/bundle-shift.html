The Shift bundles the pieces a restaurant team actually wears and carries — a t-shirt and cap for front of house, a tote for takeout, a tumbler for the staff drink that gets three sips before the next order comes in.
Sized for a real roster
Defaults to 15 of each item, sized for a single-location team. Adjust any line with the Edit button before you order — useful when front and back of house need different counts.
Consistent branding across every piece
Your logo is applied the same way across the t-shirt, cap, tote and tumbler, so the kit reads as one uniform instead of four separate giveaways. Our team confirms placement and sizing with a free digital mockup before production.
