Five essentials, one welcome
The Day One Kit bundles the pieces a new hire actually uses in their first week — a powerbank for the commute, a bottle for the desk, a notebook and pen for onboarding sessions, all arriving together in one branded box. No separate orders to track, no mismatched logos between items.
Built for day one
Sized for a real onboarding cohort: five of the higher-cost items for the people who need them most, thirty of the everyday essentials so the whole starting class is covered. Adjust either count with the Edit button on any item before you order.
Consistent branding across every piece
Your logo is applied the same way across the powerbank, bottle, notebook, pen and box, so the kit reads as one cohesive set instead of five unrelated giveaways. Our team confirms placement and sizing with a free digital mockup before anything goes into production.
Ready to ship, ready to open
Every item is individually packed inside the Custom Welcome Mailer Box, so it arrives at a new hire’s door—or their desk on morning one—as a single, unboxing-ready package rather than a pile of loose swag.
Swap anything before you order
Not quite the right mix for your team? Use Edit on any line to change the product, quantity, or colour for that item without rebuilding the whole kit from scratch.
