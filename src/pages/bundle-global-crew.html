One kit, any address
The Global Crew bundles the pieces that hold up across a distributed team’s day — a notebook and bottle for the desk, a backpack for the commute that might not happen, a candle and sweatshirt for the home office. It’s built to ship to individual addresses, not just one office.
Built for scattered teams
Sized for a full distributed roster: 25 of each item by default. Adjust any line with the Edit button before you order — useful when regional teams need different quantities.
Consistent branding across every piece
Your logo is applied the same way across the notebook, bottle, backpack, candle and sweatshirt, so the kit reads as one cohesive set no matter which time zone it lands in. Our team confirms placement and sizing with a free digital mockup before production.
Ready to ship, ready to open
Every item is individually packed inside the Custom Gift Mailer Box, so remote teammates get a single, unboxing-ready package instead of five separate deliveries.
Swap anything before you order
Not quite the right mix for your team? Use Edit on any line to change the product, quantity, or colour for that item without rebuilding the whole kit from scratch.
