Made for launch day
The Launch Day Kit bundles the pieces that sit well on a press table — a notebook and pen for note-taking, a mug for the coffee line, all boxed together so it reads as one considered gift, not a grab bag.
Built for a full room
Sized for a real event headcount: 50 of each item by default. Adjust any line with the Edit button before you order, depending on your guest list.
Consistent branding across every piece
Your logo is applied the same way across the notebook, mug and pen, so the kit reads as one cohesive set rather than three unrelated giveaways. Our team confirms placement and sizing with a free digital mockup before production.
Ready to ship, ready to open
Every item is individually packed inside the Custom Gift Mailer Box, so it arrives at the venue — or a journalist’s desk — as a single, unboxing-ready package.
Swap anything before you order
Not quite the right mix for your event? Use Edit on any line to change the product, quantity, or colour for that item without rebuilding the whole kit from scratch.
